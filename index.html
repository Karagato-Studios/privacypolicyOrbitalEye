🛰️ OrbitalEye — Privacy Policy

Real-time satellite tracking & space situational awareness

Effective date: June 2026
Contact: karagatostudios@gmail.com

1. What OrbitalEye Does

OrbitalEye is a satellite tracking app that calculates and displays the real-time position of thousands of objects in Earth orbit using publicly available Two-Line Element (TLE) data. It offers an interactive 3D globe, an augmented reality sky view, pass predictions, and conjunction alerts.

2. Permissions We Use

PermissionRequiredPurpose
Location (GPS / Coarse)OptionalCalculates which satellites are visible from your position, predicts passes over your area, and enables the AR Sky mode. Never shared with third parties. You can use the app with an approximate default location.
CameraOptionalUsed exclusively in AR Sky mode to overlay satellite positions on the live camera feed. No images or video are captured, stored, or uploaded.
InternetRequiredDownloads orbital elements (TLE) from public sources such as CelesTrak and USSPACECOM. No account or registration is needed.
StorageRequiredCaches TLE data, your favorites, pass alerts, and preferences locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers.

3. Data We Collect

What we collect:

  • Location data — only if you grant the location permission. Used solely for satellite pass calculations and AR positioning. Not stored on our servers.
  • Advertising ID — if AdMob ads are shown (see section 4).

What we DO NOT collect:

  • ✗ Name, email address, or account credentials
  • ✗ Phone number, contacts, or health data
  • ✗ Photos, videos, or microphone audio
  • ✗ Browsing history or personal identifiers beyond the advertising ID

4. Advertising (AdMob)

This app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve personalized or contextual ads:

  • Device advertising ID (e.g. Google Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system version
  • General location (city-level, not precise GPS)
  • Ad interactions (clicks, views)

If you prefer not to receive personalized ads, you can opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

For more information on how Google processes advertising data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

OrbitalEye integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising (see section 4)
  • CelesTrak / USSPACECOM — public TLE orbital data (no user data sent)
  • Google Play Services Location — optional GPS location
  • Android CameraX — optional AR camera stream (local only)

6. Data Storage & Retention

All data (TLE cache, favorites, pass alerts, preferences) is stored locally on your device. Data is retained until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. The TLE cache is updated periodically (typically every 1–3 hours).

We do not operate external servers that store personal data. Your information stays on your device.

7. Legal Basis (EU/EEA Users)

We process your data based on:

  • Consent — you grant location and camera permissions, and accept this notice
  • Legitimate interest — providing core satellite tracking functionality

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Revoke permissions at any time (Android Settings → Apps → OrbitalEye → Permissions)
  • Delete all app data (Android Settings → Apps → OrbitalEye → Storage → Clear data)
  • Uninstall the app
  • Opt out of ad personalization (device settings)

For questions or data requests: karagatostudios@gmail.com

9. Children

OrbitalEye is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the app and, if material, we may request new acceptance.

1. What StellarMap Pro Does

StellarMap Pro is an astronomy app that displays an interactive star chart, identifies celestial objects (stars, planets, constellations, deep-sky objects), provides ephemeris data, and offers an augmented reality sky view.

2. Permissions We Use

PermissionRequiredPurpose
Location (GPS / Coarse)OptionalCalculates the visible sky from your position for accurate star maps and AR alignment. Not shared with third parties. You can browse the star chart with a default location.
CameraOptionalUsed in AR Sky mode to overlay constellations and objects on the live camera feed. No images or video are captured, stored, or uploaded.
InternetRequiredDownloads star catalogs, ephemeris updates, and astronomical data. No account or registration is needed.
StorageRequiredCaches star catalogs, observation logs, favorites, and app preferences locally on your device.

3. Data We Collect

What we collect:

  • Location data — only if you grant the location permission. Used solely for sky chart positioning and AR alignment. Not stored on our servers.
  • Advertising ID — if AdMob ads are shown (see section 4).

What we DO NOT collect:

  • ✗ Name, email address, or account credentials
  • ✗ Phone number, contacts, or health data
  • ✗ Photos, videos, or microphone audio
  • ✗ Browsing history or personal identifiers beyond the advertising ID
  • ✗ Observation data or personal notes (stored only on your device)

4. Advertising (AdMob)

This app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve personalized or contextual ads:

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising
